Features

Core Gameplay

Combat

Real-time combat with firearms, melee weapons, and cybernetic tools. Use the environment to your advantage.

Cybernetics

Augment your character with cybernetic upgrades. Each one changes how you approach combat, stealth, and dialogue.

Stealth

Disable security systems, avoid patrols, and slip past obstacles undetected.

Branching Story

Your decisions determine how the story unfolds. Ally with different factions and deal with the consequences.

World

Neo-Seattle

Explore distinct districts of a cyberpunk city, from neon-lit streets to corporate towers and underground tunnels.

NPCs

Characters with their own agendas. Build relationships, gather info, or manipulate people to get what you need.

Side Content

Take on side jobs, uncover hidden lore, and find upgrades scattered throughout the city.

Details

The world is filled with details that tell stories. Pay attention to your surroundings.
