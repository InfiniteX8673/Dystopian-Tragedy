# **GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT: DYSTOPIAN TRAGEDY**

**Project Title:** Dystopian Tragedy — Part 1: “The Rings”

**Timeline:** 3012 – 3030

**Setting:** The Island / The Rings

---

## **1. CORE STORY & BACKSTORY**

### **The Origin**

* **The Tragedy:** Jesina (mother of the protagonist, Chris Smith) was married to **The Shadow** (Steven Benson, known as **BOB**). Jesina died during Chris’s birth.
* **The Abandonment:** Overcome by cold indifference or malice, Shadow abandoned the newborn Chris in the streets.
* **The Rescue:** **Alara**, who had recently escaped being kidnapped by Shadow (her ex-boyfriend who intended to kill her), heard the cries of a baby while fleeing. Her heart ached, and she adopted Chris as her own.

### **The Protagonist: Chris Smith**

* **Personality:** Chris puts on a "tough guy" persona to command respect and fear, driven by a deep desire to be the leader figure and protector of his home.
* **Appearance:** Heavily integrated with futuristic chips, implants, and cyber parts.

## **3. GAMEPLAY MECHANICS & SYSTEMS**

### **Combat & Gear**

* **Weapons:** Players utilize high-tech weaponry including **Plasma Cannons**, **Tesla Coils**, and **Hand Cannons**.
* **Cybernetics:** Deep integration of cyber-parts that affect gameplay and movement.
* **Smooth Gameplay:** Focus on fluid movement, parkour (sprint, vault, slide), and "Cyber Dash."

### **Special Mechanics**

* **Hacking:** Mini-games involving circuit matching and timed decryption to uncover "The Sky Lie" (Project Aurora).
* **Stealth:** Avoiding Shadow’s drone patrols.
* **Constraints:** High-stakes "Play Scenes" (like Alara’s escape) where the player is not told the time limit and may only have one chance to succeed.

---

## **4. MISSION BREAKDOWN (ACT I - ACT III)**

### **Act I: Ignition**

* **Mission 1 (Prologue):** Play as Alara. Escape Shadow's agents in Ring 2 and find baby Chris.
* **Mission 2 (Safehouse):** Tutorial on scavenging (crates) and cybernetic upgrades.
* **Mission 3 (Street Training):** Teen Chris trains with Dave (Red Ant). First combat with Red Slag District.

### **Act II: Descent**

* **Mission 4 (The Discovery):** Infiltrate Ring 3 Data Center. Uncover that the sky is an illusion maintained by the Tower.
* **Mission 5 (Ring 4):** Navigate the ruins. Discovery of the "Abandoned Shoes" (Fog victims).
* **Mission 6 (The Tower):** Infiltrate the Island. AI detection: *"Threat Level: Critical."*

### **Act III: The Fall**

* **Mission 7 (The Chase):** Escape to the Edge of the Island.
* **Mission 8 (Betrayal):** Confront Kade at the edge.
* **Choice A:** Jump voluntarily (Path of Hope).
* **Choice B:** Kade pushes Chris (Path of Betrayal).
