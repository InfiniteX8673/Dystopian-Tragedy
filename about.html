The World

The Island & The Rings, 3012 – 3030

The world is divided into Rings encircling a central Island, each controlled by powerful factions with their own agendas. Gangs vie for territory, technology is both weapon and lifeline, and the sky above hides a terrible secret.

The Story

Your name is Chris Smith. Your mother, Jesina, died giving birth to you — married to a man known only as The Shadow. Overcome by malice, Shadow abandoned you in the streets as a newborn. But fate had other plans: Alara, fleeing from Shadow's own clutches, heard your cries and chose to raise you as her own.

Now a teenager heavily integrated with cybernetic parts, Chris puts on a tough exterior to survive the Rings — hiding a deep desire to be the protector of his home. When a mission to infiltrate Ring 3's Data Center uncovers that the sky itself is an illusion maintained by a tower known as Project Aurora, everything changes. The path forward leads through the ruins, to the Island, and to a final confrontation with Shadow himself.

Your Choices Matter

Every decision shapes the story. Navigate gang politics, choose who to trust, and when the moment comes — jump into the unknown, or be pushed. There are no clear heroes here, just people trying to survive.

Mechanics

Combat

High-tech weaponry — Plasma Cannons, Tesla Coils, and Hand Cannons in real-time action.

Cybernetics

Deep integration of cyber-parts, implants, and chips that affect gameplay and movement.

Movement

Fluid parkour — sprint, vault, slide, and Cyber Dash through the Rings.

Hacking

Circuit-matching and timed decryption mini-games to uncover the truth behind Project Aurora.
